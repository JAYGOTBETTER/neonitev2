BEAST BOY for Injustice 2  (v0.1.0)
===================================

Pick CHEETAH and you fight as Beast Boy.

  * Beast Boy fights in a green Lantern-light glow.
  * GORILLA SLAM: press the assist button (gamepad: click the RIGHT STICK,
    keyboard: P). Beast Boy morphs into Gorilla Grodd, who slams in, then
    Beast Boy snaps back. It recharges after a few seconds.

Play it in Versus against the CPU, Training or Multiverse. Single player only:
don't use it online.

Grodd and the green light come from your own copy of Injustice 2; nothing from
the game is included in this mod.

Settings: BeastBoy.ini (next to this file). Change the keyboard key, how long
the gorilla stays, or set Enabled=0 to turn the mod off.
Log: BeastBoy.log (next to this file) shows what the mod did in the last session.

Needs the current Steam version of Injustice 2. On another version the mod
says so when the game starts and stays off.
